Mohr's Circle — 2D Plane Stress

Enter the coordinates of points V and H, then see the plane stress state with its normal stresses and strains.

Inputs

V point — vertical face
H point — horizontal face
Shear on V and H must be equal in size and opposite in sign (τH = −τV). The average is being used.

Stress in MPa. Shear τ is positive when it turns the element clockwise, and is plotted upward.

Material (for strains)

Plane stress, linear-elastic, isotropic. G = E / (2(1+ν)).

How to read it

  1. Plot V(σV, τV) and H(σH, τH); join them — that line is a diameter.
  2. Centre C = (σV+σH)/2, radius R = distance C–V.
  3. Principal stresses σ1,2 = C ± R (where τ = 0).
  4. Max in-plane shear = R, at σ = C.

Mohr's circle

V (vertical face) H (horizontal face) Principal / max shear
