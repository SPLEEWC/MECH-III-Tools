MECH III Tools

Interactive tools for Mechanics of Materials. Pick one to get started.

Mohr's Circle — Stress

2D plane stress: enter the V and H face stresses (σ, τ).

  • Principal stresses and maximum shear stress
  • Stresses on an arbitrary joint plane
  • Resulting strains from E and ν
Open tool →

Mohr's Circle — Strain

Choose a strain rosette (rectangle, delta or star) and enter the gauge strains ε1, ε2, ε3.

  • Circle drawn step by step using McClintock's method
  • Principal strains and maximum shear strain
  • Strains on an arbitrary joint plane
  • Corresponding stresses from E and ν
Open tool →
Shear stress and shear strain are positive clockwise in both tools.
