Mohr's Circle Tutor

A step-by-step self-learning guide to drawing Mohr's circle for stress and strain. Need a quick calculator instead? Open the stress or strain tool, or go back to MECH III Tools.

What would you like to learn?

How this tutor works

  • Pick a worked example or type in your own values.
  • Follow one small step at a time — the circle is built up on screen as you go.
  • Have a sheet of graph paper ready and draw along with each step.
  • Type your answer into Check yourself boxes to get instant feedback.
  • Stuck? Press Show working to see the full calculation with your numbers.
  • Use the step buttons, Next/Back, or the ← → keys to move around.

Sign convention used throughout (same as the calculator): tension / extension is positive; shear stress and shear strain are positive when they turn the element clockwise, and are plotted upward. With this convention, a rotation on the circle is in the same direction as the rotation of the plane on the element.
