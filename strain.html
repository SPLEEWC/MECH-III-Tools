Mohr's Circle — 2D Plane Strain

Choose a strain rosette and enter its three gauge readings, then see the strain state with its principal and maximum shear strains, and the corresponding stresses.

Strain rosette

1. Choose the rosette
2. Enter the gauge strains (µε)

Gauge angles are measured counter-clockwise from x. Strain in µε (10−6). Shear strain γ is positive clockwise; the vertical axis plots γ/2, upward.

Joint

The joint plane is the reference plane rotated by the angle in the chosen direction. Its strains are the point rotated by 2×angle the same way on the circle.

Material (for stresses)

Plane stress, linear-elastic, isotropic: σx = E(εx+νεy)/(1−ν²), τ = Gγ, G = E / (2(1+ν)).

How to read it

  1. The three gauge readings fix the circle: εθ = C + R·cos(2θ + φ) fitted at each gauge angle. V (εx, γ/2) and H (εy, −γ/2) are the ends of a diameter.
  2. Centre C = (εV+εH)/2, radius R = distance C–V.
  3. Principal strains ε1,2 = C ± R (where γ/2 = 0).
  4. Max in-plane shear strain γmax = 2R, at ε = C.
