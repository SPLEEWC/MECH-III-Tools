Mohr's Circle — 2D Plane Strain

Enter the coordinates of points V and H (ε, γ/2), then see the strain state with its principal and maximum shear strains, and the corresponding stresses.

Inputs

V point — vertical face
H point — horizontal face
Shear strain on V and H must be equal in size and opposite in sign (γH/2 = −γV/2). The average is being used.

Strain in µε (10−6). Shear strain γ is positive clockwise; the vertical axis plots γ/2, upward.

Joint

The joint plane is the reference plane rotated by the angle in the chosen direction. Its strains are the point rotated by 2×angle the same way on the circle.

Material (for stresses)

Plane stress, linear-elastic, isotropic: σx = E(εx+νεy)/(1−ν²), τ = Gγ, G = E / (2(1+ν)).

How to read it

  1. Plot V(εV, γV/2) and H(εH, γH/2); join them — that line is a diameter.
  2. Centre C = (εV+εH)/2, radius R = distance C–V.
  3. Principal strains ε1,2 = C ± R (where γ/2 = 0).
  4. Max in-plane shear strain γmax = 2R, at ε = C.

Plane strain state — deformed element, strains & stresses

Green lines: principal planes P and Q; teal dash-dot lines: maximum shear planes S+ and S− (45° from them); purple dashed: joint. Dashed orange outline: deformed element, greatly exaggerated. Solid arrows: the corresponding stresses (blue = normal, red = shear). Shear strain γ and shear stress are both positive clockwise. Strains are in µε (10−6); γ in µrad; stresses in N/mm².
